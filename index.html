02 — Kimma

Co-founder and CRO

Open-source HR. We raised $100,000 to build it.

I’m co-founder and CRO, with Kyriakos Tsourekas and Iskhak Tazhibaev. Kyriakos took gold at the International Mathematical Olympiad at sixteen. Iskhak led Kyrgyzstan at the International Olympiad in AI.

Kimma is the employee record, the review cycle, hiring, learning, and the moves inside a company — one system, instead of a separate product for each. It is open source on purpose. A license on the record would be charging for the wrong thing.

A team describes the workflow in the words they already use. Kimma rewrites the process to match, and agents run it — the scheduling, the handoff, the follow-up — without an implementation project or a ticket to a vendor.

The software stays free. The price sits on the AI.

03 — Work

Where I've been building.

A summer on product in San Francisco, and engineering in Athens at the fastest-growing startup in Greece.

  1. Summer 2026San Francisco

    Product, Caretta

    YC W26 · Former

    Caretta is a realtime copilot for sales calls. It listens while the meeting is still going and puts the answer, the objection, and the account story in front of the rep — the thing they would otherwise pause to ask a sales engineer. I worked on the product with the team in San Francisco for the summer.

  2. Mar — Jul 2026Athens

    Software engineer, Techmellon

    Fastest-growing startup in Greece

    Techmellon builds AI infrastructure for regulated industries — healthcare, insurance, heavy industry — and keeps those systems running where every action is audited. I got the job by winning the Hustle Hours hackathon. With Robert Ryan and Ioannis Bilitsos, we took first place with property-management software meant to do the work a manager usually does by hand.

  3. Jun — Jul 2025Athens

    Software engineering intern, OpenCouncil

    Civic software

    OpenCouncil turns municipal council meetings into transcripts, plain-language decisions, and a note when the decision is about your street. A summer on software that lets a resident skip the three-hour session and still know what the city did.
